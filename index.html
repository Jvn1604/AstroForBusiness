Frequently asked questions

What counts as an "entertainment outlet"?

Discotheques, nightclubs, KTV lounges, karaoke, lounges, pubs, bars, bistros, beer gardens, multi-entertainment outlets and restaurants with a pub or bar facility. These outlets use the BizOne 1,000 Entertainment plan.

What does "pax" mean?

Pax refers to the customer capacity of your premises. Each plan includes a number of pax; beyond that, a per-pax monthly charge applies (RM3–RM10 depending on plan).

Are prices inclusive of SST?

No. All package prices shown exclude 8% SST imposed by the government. Our price calculator shows the SST amount separately.

How long is the contract? Can I cancel early?

All BizOne plans are subject to a 12-month contract for both new customers and existing Astro customers upgrading. Early termination is governed by Astro's subscription terms — see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

Am I eligible for BizOne Rewards?

If you are a new F&B customer subscribing to BizOne 800, 1,000 or 1,200 during the campaign period with a minimum monthly nett subscription of RM800, you will be assessed automatically after activation. One reward per account.
