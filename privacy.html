Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SVC Global (Registration No. 202203205796 (TR0269567-V)) ("we", "us") handles your personal data in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) of Malaysia. A Bahasa Malaysia version is available on request.

1. What we collect

We only collect what we need to answer your enquiry:

DataWhy we need itRequired?
NameTo address youYes
Phone numberTo call or WhatsApp you back with a quoteYes
Business name & outlet typeTo recommend the correct Astro Business planYes
Plan of interest & messageTo understand your needsNo
Marketing preferenceTo send promotions only if you opt inNo

We do not ask for your IC number, bank details or passwords on this website. Please do not send them to us through the website or WhatsApp.

2. How the enquiry form works

This website does not store form submissions. When you press "Send via WhatsApp", your details are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device, which you choose whether to send. Once sent, the message is processed by WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) under its own privacy policy, and received by us.

3. How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To process your subscription with Astro (MEASAT Broadcast Network Systems Sdn Bhd) if you decide to sign up. Astro's own privacy notice then also applies.
  • To send you promotions only if you ticked the optional marketing box.

4. Who we share it with

We share your details only with Astro to process your subscription, and with service providers we need to run our business (e.g. WhatsApp, our phone provider). We never sell your personal data.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not result in a subscription are deleted within 12 months. Customer records are kept for as long as the contract requires and Malaysian law requires (e.g. tax records, up to 7 years).

6. Third-party tools

This website loads no third-party scripts, trackers, advertising pixels, web fonts or SDKs. All files are served from our own domain. WhatsApp and phone links only open when you click them.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS with strict security headers and a Content Security Policy. No personal data is stored on the web server.

8. Children

Our services are for businesses. Enquiries must be made by persons aged 18 or above. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has contacted us, tell us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Under the PDPA you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, withdraw consent, and stop processing for direct marketing.

10. Delete my data / unsubscribe

To delete your data or stop receiving promotions, do any one of the following:

  • Reply STOP to any promotional WhatsApp or SMS from us and you will be unsubscribed immediately.
  • Call 03-7490 4608 and ask for "data deletion".
  • Write to: Data Protection Officer, SVC Global, 10-1, Jln Opera B/U2, TTDI Jaya, 40150 Shah Alam, Selangor Darul Ehsan.

We will confirm and complete deletion within 21 days, except for records we must keep by law (we will tell you which).
