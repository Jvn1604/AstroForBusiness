Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. We do not take payments on this website

No payment is collected through this website. Subscription fees are billed and collected by Astro directly. We will never ask you to transfer money to a personal bank account.

2. Contract tenure

All Astro BizOne packages are subject to a 12-month minimum contract, for both new customers and existing Astro customers upgrading.

3. Before installation

If you change your mind before installation, tell us as soon as possible on 03-7490 4608 and we will cancel your application with Astro. Any deposit paid to Astro is refunded by Astro in line with its policy.

4. Early termination

Ending the contract within the 12 months may incur early termination charges and/or decoder return obligations under Astro's subscription terms. We will explain these in writing before you sign.

5. Billing disputes and refunds

Astro handles billing disputes and refunds. We are happy to help you raise them. Astro Business support: 03-9544 7566.

6. Fees we disclose upfront

  • Monthly package fee (before 8% SST)
  • Additional pax charge above the included customer count
  • RM300/month per additional decoder
  • Add-on channels (optional, priced separately)

There are no other dealer fees.
